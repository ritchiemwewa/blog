If you know me from my dev or open source work, you probably know me as **rly0nheart**. You may also have no idea how to say it. It's "lionheart." For years I was the only one who knew that, which is part of the problem, I guess.

I've made the decision to start using **ritchiemwewa** for my online accounts/handles. **rly0nheart** carried me through my early projects and a lot of learning, but at some point, it started to look less professional than I'd like. I think a name people can read, say, and take seriously, makes more sense.

> [!Info] PS
> I have nothing against leet haxor names. I've had rly0nheart since 2019. For all intents and purposes, *I'm something of a leet haxor myself.*

![leet haxor](https://imagepaste.org/i/w28vg923.jpg)

It feels like I'm letting go of something very important to me, and that feeling sucks, but this is mostly about consistency, and professionalism. My new site now lives at [mwewa.org](https://mwewa.org), and I want one name everywhere I show up online. rly0nheart.com was consistent with the handle @rly0nheart, but as I've mentioned already, it started to seem less professional.

So, I'll be moving my accounts over to **ritchiemwewa** as I go.

[rly0nheart.com](https://rly0nheart.com) will keep redirecting to [mwewa.org](https://mwewa.org) until the domain expires sometime next year. Please treat mwewa.org as the source of truth and update any links or bookmarks you may have.

Here's to new beginnings! And to never spelling out "lionheart" again?
